1. 我們收集咩資料？ / What we collect

本 App 唔會收集、上傳或傳送個人資料到開發者伺服器或其他第三方伺服器。App 唔需要登入帳戶，亦唔含廣告或追蹤 SDK。

English: This app does not collect, upload, or transmit personal data to the developer or third-party servers. No account, no ads, no tracking SDKs.

2. 本機儲存 / On-device storage

你輸入嘅筆記標題、正文、標籤、搜尋相關文字，以及你附加嘅細相片（壓縮後）同相片說明，會用裝置上的本機資料庫（Room）同本機檔案儲存。呢啲資料淨係留喺你部手機／平板，開發者收唔到。

English: Notes (title, body, tags), small photo attachments (compressed) and captions are stored only in on-device Room storage and local files. The developer cannot access them.

3. 備份／還原 / Backup & restore

你可以自行將資料匯出成 ZIP（內含可讀 JSON 同相片），或由 ZIP 匯入還原。檔案去向完全由你決定。匯入會覆蓋本機全部知識庫資料，請先自行備份。

English: You may export a ZIP (readable JSON + photos) or import to restore. You control where files go. Import replaces the entire local library—back up first.

4. 相片 / Photos

相片只喺你主動由相簿揀選後，先會複製／壓縮存入本 App 私有儲存。App 唔會掃描或上傳你成個相簿。相片說明可用於本機搜尋。

English: Photos are copied/compressed into the app’s private storage only after you pick them. The app does not scan or upload your whole gallery. Captions are used for on-device search only.

5. Ask／推理 / Ask (keyword matching)

「Ask」功能係本機關鍵字配對，唔係雲端 AI，亦唔會將你嘅問題或筆記送出裝置。

English: Ask uses on-device keyword matching—not cloud AI. Questions and notes never leave the device.

6. 權限同網絡 / Permissions & network

目前版本唔要求 INTERNET 權限，亦唔使用廣告識別碼（AD_ID）。讀取相片經系統相片選擇器，由你每次授權／揀選。若日後加入雲端同步或其他網絡功能，我哋會更新本政策並喺商店說明。

English: Current builds do not require INTERNET and do not use AD_ID. Photo access is via the system picker when you choose. Cloud features would require an updated policy and store listing.

7. 資料出售同分享 / Sale & sharing

開發者唔會出售、出租或向廣告商分享你嘅個人資料——因為目前建置根本冇將你嘅筆記／相片上傳到開發者伺服器。

English: We do not sell or share your data with advertisers; the current build does not upload notes or photos to our servers.

8. 資料刪除 / Deletion

你可以喺 App 內刪除筆記／相片，清除 App 資料，或卸載 App，以移除本機內容。

English: Delete notes/photos in-app, clear app storage, or uninstall to remove local data.

9. 兒童私隱 / Children

本 App 係生產力／工具類應用，唔針對兒童收集資料；實際上亦唔向開發者傳送任何使用者資料。

English: This is a productivity utility; we do not target children and do not receive user data.
